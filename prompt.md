# The prompt

Fifth game in the harsh-critic-loop series (after
[NEONOID](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neonoid),
[NEON MINER](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neonminer),
[NEODROID](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neodroid) and
[NEON DASH](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neondash)). The destination the
harness has been walking toward since the Manic Miner bug: levels as
theorems.

```
Build a Lemmings tribute at the level of a modern commercial remake.
Single-file browser game, zero assets. Original levels and creatures —
the real Lemmings (DMA Design, 1991) is copyrighted. Pixel-destructible
terrain, a drip-feed hatch, a save quota, and seven classic skills:
climber, floater, bomber, blocker, builder, basher, digger.

One owner writes the whole game. Then /loop harsh sub-agent critics: the
three visual lenses plus a LEMMINGS-FIDELITY critic judging skill
mechanics against the 1991 canon.

The harness requirement is absolute: EVERY level ships with an authored
solution that the harness replays headlessly and verifies (quota met),
plus a null run with no skills that must FAIL (skills are load-bearing).
A level without a machine-checked proof does not ship. Assignment event
logs make every proof auditable. Grade against the commercial bar, call
out fixes that didn't land in the pixels, loop until plateau, report the
honest number.
```
